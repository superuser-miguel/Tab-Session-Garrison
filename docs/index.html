    Search today

    Quotes mean exactly what you typed.

    Plain words can match anywhere in a session. Put them in quotes and they have to appear together, inside one tab title, session name or tab-group name.

    Words

    stop light

    Finds sessions where “stop” and “light” both appear, even in different tabs. Good for casting a wide net.

    Phrase

    "stop light"

    Finds only the tab that actually says “stop light”. A phrase never spans two tabs. Results update while you type, even before the closing quote.

    Tab groups

    itinerary

    Group names are searchable too, so typing a group's name surfaces every session that contains it. Matches are highlighted in the list and the tab list.

    Search roadmap

    Where search is going.

    Each step ships on its own. The order follows how people actually look for things: by a site they half remember, or a few words from a page title.

    1. Phrases and group names Live · 0.3.4

      Exact "quoted phrases" that stay inside one tab, searchable tab-group names, and highlighted matches.

    2. Search inside links Next

      Many sites give every tab the same title. Five tabs all called “Instagram” only differ by their address, so reels or a profile name should find instagram.com/some_profile/reels/. Full links will be searched, without the https:// and www. noise, with title matches ranked first.

    3. Tags, exclusions and filters Next

      Narrow a search down instead of scrolling:

      • Your own tags become searchable.
      • -word leaves out sessions that match it.
      • site: url: tag: group: title: name: search one place only.

    4. Fuzzy matching, best result first Planned

      In the spirit of fzf: ghb finds GitHub, exact hits rank above fuzzy ones, the best session floats to the top and is pre-selected, and Enter opens it. Clear the box and your own sort order comes back.

    5. Why did it match? Planned

      Each result says what matched (“tab: Stop light timing…”, “link: github.com/…”, “group: Projects”), the detail pane jumps to the matching tabs, and a count shows how many tabs matched.

    The rest of the toolkit

    Built for big profiles.

    Once you've found it, everything else stays fast.

    Failures you can see

    If saving ever fails, a red ! on the toolbar icon tells you, instead of auto-save quietly stopping.

    Bulk actions, one undo

    Open or delete dozens of sessions at once, and get them all back with a single undo.

    Tab-group aware

    Named, coloured groups are saved and restored, and shown on every session.

    Fast at any size

    The list appears in about a quarter of a second, even with 3,500 saved sessions.
